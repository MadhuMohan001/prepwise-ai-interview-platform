# PrepWise - AI Interview Preparation Platform

## Problem
Freshers struggle to prepare for technical interviews
and evaluate their answers.

## Target Users
Students and entry-level job seekers.

## Solution
An AI-powered platform that generates technical
interview questions and evaluates user answers.

## Core Features
1. Dashboard
2. Topic and difficulty selection
3. AI question generation
4. Answer submission
5. AI answer evaluation
6. Feedback and scores
7. Practice history

## Technology
React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Gemini API,
Vercel Functions and Vitest.

## Deployment
Vercel.
